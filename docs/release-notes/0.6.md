# Release 0.6 — Rewards

Closed on 2026-10-20.

## What this release is

The game. Every completed task now brings a reward, the family opens them together on Sunday, and what comes out of each one joins the person's collection.

## What now exists

- **A reward for every completed task.** Completing a task still takes two taps. The reward goes to the vault, and its look already hints at how rare it is.
- **The vault**, a place on the map, keeps each person's own unopened rewards, grouped by week. Nobody sees anybody else's.
- **Opening on Sunday.** The week's rewards open from Sunday on. Anything from a past week opens at any time, so a missed Sunday is simply opened on Monday. Opening the same reward on two phones gives one collectible, never two.
- **The party.** What comes out of a reward can join the person's party (up to 5) at once, or wait. When the party is full, the person chooses who leaves for the reserve. Anything left waiting moves to the reserve the next day. Nothing is ever lost or deleted.
- **Reward odds.** In management, an adult can tune how often each kind of reward comes out, for each task difficulty.
- **The pirate theme.** The world can now be a pirate world: its own names, the lighthouse sage, a catalogue of collectibles, and its own app icon. The art is a first version with emoji; the final art comes in 0.7.

## Good to know

- Tasks completed before this release bring no reward. Rewards start with the first task completed after it.
- How rare a reward is gets decided when the task is completed, not when it is opened.
