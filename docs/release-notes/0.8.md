# Release 0.8 — Usability

Closed on 2026-10-23.

## What this release is

The game gets easier to use. The same few ideas are now done the same way on every screen, and the adults get a better view of the family.

## What now exists

- **One form for tasks.** Creating a task is one screen: once or repeating, for one person or for everyone at once, with a difficulty, an optional question, and (for adults) the "big" option. Big tasks can also be one-offs, planned on any day from today.
- **One task card.** The board and the check-in show the same card: the title, then small chips for how it repeats and when it is planned. A new task has a "novo" marker.
- **The character asks, and you can undo.** A task with a question is completed first and then the character asks it, with "Sim" and "Não". After "Feito" there are four seconds to change your mind with "Desfazer".
- **An alert for the board.** The parrot tells you when a task is for today, was planned for a day that has passed, is coming tomorrow, or is new to you.
- **The crew.** The tavern holds 10 places (an adult can change that), joining a full crew asks who goes to the tavern, and collectibles can be brought back from it.
- **Names as labels.** Every collectible shows its name as a tag on its picture, and tapping the tag renames it.
- **The adults' view.** "Tarefas por perfil" shows a profile's open tasks and what it finished in the last 7 days. It has no totals or comparisons.
- **One look and feel.** The same top bar on every screen, larger touch targets, clearer text, and no animation if your phone asks for none.
- **A simpler profile picker.** Each profile shows its emoji, with the name alone.

## Good to know

- The board only shows tasks. They are completed in the check-in, at the lighthouse.
- A task with a question takes one more tap, because the question comes after "Feito".
- The product owner must publish the new Firestore rules before devices can create a big one-off task.
