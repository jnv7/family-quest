# Release 0.7 — Home, showcase and art

Closed on 2026-10-21.

## What this release is

The family gets a face. Each person has a home, the latest collectibles are on show for everyone, and the pirate world has its final look.

## What now exists

- **A map of an island.** The pirate world is now a painted island with a lighthouse, a cave, a tavern, a parrot at the dock and paths between them. Every place is drawn for the game; the pieces from the Kenney packs are free to use (CC0), and `CREDITS.md` lists every image.
- **Your boat.** Each child has a small boat moored at the pier, and the adults share the big ship with golden sails. A person chooses a symbol (any emoji, with the theme's suggestions first and then the animals and plants), and it appears on the sail of their boat, on the map and at home.
- **The home.** It shows your boat, your symbol, your party and the collectibles you have not decided about yet. Collectibles can be renamed and, in one tap, sent to the tavern.
- **The tavern.** The latest collectibles that people sent away are on show here, for everyone, with no owner and no counts. An adult can rename any of them if a name is not right. A person can put one of their own back into the party from here.
- **Clearer tasks.** The tasks are cards, with the button to complete them in front. "I think I did better" is now a line of text that tells you to talk to your parents.
- **The alert.** When a planned task is for today or tomorrow, the parrot shows a speech bubble.

## Good to know

- The collectibles still show emoji; their final art is not part of this release.
- Once sent to the tavern, a collectible no longer appears on your home. You find it again in the tavern.
