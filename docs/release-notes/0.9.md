# Release 0.9 — Rewards that last

Closed on 2026-10-23.

## What this release is

Rewards stop depending on the day of the week. A task earns tokens, tokens buy rewards whenever you like, and nothing is spent twice, even when phones are offline. A task made by mistake can be retired, and the whole game can start over.

## What now exists

- **Tokens.** Finishing a task earns tokens by how hard it is (1, 2 or 4 to start with). After "Feito" you see them as a row of small icons. A task a child invents always earns 1. An adult can change the values in management.
- **Spending in the vault.** The vault shows your tokens at the top, and nowhere else. Below it is the depth: three levels, from the entrance to the deep end, each with its price. Choose one you can afford, tap "Abrir por N", and the prize is yours at once. A level you cannot afford is dimmed. In the pirate world the depth is a cave.
- **No double spending.** If two phones of the same person are offline and both spend the same tokens, only one purchase stands. The other says the tokens were spent on another device, and nothing is lost.
- **What you find.** The chests earned before this release are still there, under the cave, and open for free on any day.
- **The crew, as buttons.** At home the crew is a grid of small buttons with each creature's picture and name. Tap one to open its card: rename it, send it to the tavern, or close.
- **Taking from the tavern.** Anyone can take any creature from the tavern into their own crew, whoever sent it there. It keeps its name and becomes theirs. If two people take it at once, the first one wins.
- **Retiring a task.** A task made by mistake has a "⋯" at its corner. Adults can retire any open task. A child can only retire one they made for themselves. It asks once.
- **Bigger crews and smaller ones.** An adult can change the size of the crew from 1 to 10. Lowering it never takes anyone out.
- **Starting over.** The last thing in management is a red button that restarts the world: it deletes all tasks (the repeating ones too), chests, creatures, tokens and crews, and keeps the people and their phones. It asks twice, and it cannot be undone.
- **Pirate words.** In the pirate world the tokens are torches and the creatures are "marujos". There are 8 creatures of each level.

## Good to know

- The rules in Firebase changed in this release, and the product owner has to publish them again whenever they change. Phones that have not updated yet keep working with the old way until they do.
- There are no weeks any more in the vault, and no opening day.
- Restarting needs a connection.
