# Release 0.5 — Children pilot (7 days), without rewards: plan

**Start only after the product owner closes 0.4** ([0.4.md](0.4.md)). The developer works on `release/0.5`, created from `main`.

This release builds **no new features**. It is the gate of the project: **if this pilot fails, 0.6 (rewards) is not built** (`ROADMAP.md`). It tests whether the children use the app for its own sake, before any reward exists.

**Exit criteria** (`ROADMAP.md`). All four must hold:

1. The children use the app on at least 5 of 7 days without being called.
2. Completing a task takes less than 10 seconds.
3. Nobody describes it as "more work".
4. The number of tasks the children create does not grow artificially.

The roles, triage, fixes as patches (`0.5.x`) and privacy rules are the same as in [0.4.md](0.4.md). The privacy rules matter even more here: in the repository the children are "Child 1", "Child 2", with no names, ages, school or routines.

## [ ] A. Before day 1 (product owner)

1. **Phones.** Each child's phone has the app installed from the join link, with their own profile chosen. Adults keep using theirs.
2. **Tasks.** Each child has a few recurring tasks they already do (2 to 4 daily, 1 weekly), with a question on at least one. Keep the load the same as before the app: the pilot measures the app, not new chores.
3. **Talking to the children:**
   - explain the app once, in a few minutes, and show how to complete a task;
   - say there are no rewards yet;
   - after that, **do not remind them to open it**. That is what criterion 1 measures.
4. **Stopwatch.** Prepare a phone stopwatch for criterion 2.
5. **Check the limit.** `childDailyTaskLimit` is 3 by default; leave it as it is.

## [ ] B. During the 7 days

- **Daily log**, kept outside the repository, one row per child per day:

  | Date | Child | Used without being called? | Was called? | Tasks completed | Ad hoc tasks they created | "No" answers | Talk flags | Notes (what they said, friction) |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

  "Used without being called" counts only if no adult prompted them that day. If an adult did, the day does not count for criterion 1, even if they then used it.

- **Speed (criterion 2).** On three different days per child, time a completion without the child knowing it is being timed. Start when the child taps the app icon, and stop when the task shows as done. Write down the seconds.
- **Talk flags.** When a child uses "I think I did better", have that conversation; it is part of the design. Note only that it happened.
- **Bugs and ideas** go through the same triage as 0.4. Children's suggestions ("I want a…") are ideas: write them down, but do not build them.

## [ ] C. Day 7

1. **Criterion 3, a short conversation** with each child, with open questions, not leading ones:
   - "How was it to use the app this week?"
   - "Was it easier or harder than before?"
   - "Would you keep using it?"

   Write down their words, in the private log only. If any child says or clearly means "more work" (in any wording), criterion 3 fails.
2. **Criterion 4.** From the log (cross-check with management), list the ad hoc tasks each child created per day:
   - **Artificial growth** means the count rises day after day, or the tasks are trivial ones made to have something to complete (for example, "drink water" ten times). That is a warning sign for when rewards exist.
   - **The daily limit** caps it at 3. A child hitting the limit every day counts as a warning too.
3. **Usage.** Firebase console, peak reads/writes per day, as in 0.4.
4. **Report.** Write `docs/pilots/0.5.md`, anonymised:

   ```markdown
   # Pilot 0.5 — children — report

   - Dates: <week>
   - Participants: Child 1, Child 2 (+ adults as before)

   ## Exit criteria
   | Criterion | Result | Met? |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Used ≥5 of 7 days without being called | C1: n/7, C2: n/7 | yes / no |
   | Completion < 10 s | C1: a, b, c s; C2: a, b, c s | yes / no |
   | Nobody says "more work" | summary, no quotes that identify | yes / no |
   | No artificial growth of child-created tasks | per-day counts | yes / no |

   ## Usage, friction, bugs fixed, ideas raised
   ```

5. **Decision** (product owner):
   - **all four met:** close 0.5, and the tech lead prepares the 0.6 plan (rewards: generation, `vault`, opening, `party`);
   - **any criterion fails:** 0.6 is not built as planned. The product owner decides with the tech lead whether to adjust and repeat the pilot, or to change course. Rewards must not be used to make up for an app the children do not want to use.

**Before 0.6 starts,** the product owner decides the `tierOdds` rows for the `low` and `high` levels (`SPEC.md` §13). The 0.6 plan depends on them.

## Outcome (2026-10-20)

The pilot ran and met its exit criteria, according to the product owner. By the product owner's choice, no detailed pilot report is recorded. The release was closed as `v0.5`.
