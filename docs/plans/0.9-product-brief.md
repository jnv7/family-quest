# Release 0.9 — Product brief: tokens and the `vault`

**Status: decided by the product owner (2026-10-23).** Written for the tech lead, who turns it into `docs/plans/0.9.md`, an ADR, the `SPEC.md` changes and the rules changes.

This brief **replaces "Reward pieces"** (`SPEC.md` §6, decision D5 in `docs/plans/0.8.md`). "Retiring a task" stays in 0.9 exactly as specified in `SPEC.md` §6.

Goal: a reward model built on **choice and managing a scarce resource**. A person earns tokens by completing tasks and decides how to spend them: open a cheap reward now, or save for a rarer one.

Everything below uses the neutral glossary (`SPEC.md` §3). The pirate names are given only as an example of a theme.

---

## 1. Glossary changes

| Name in code | Role | Pirate theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `token` (**new**) | Unit earned by completing a task and spent in the `vault` | Tocha |
| `vault` (**changed**) | Place where a person spends tokens to open a reward of a chosen tier | Gruta do tesouro |
| `reward` (**changed**) | What is opened in the `vault`; it has a `tier` and yields one collectible | Baú |

No `market` place and no `key` concept: both were considered and dropped. The map keeps its five places.

## 2. Earning tokens

- Completing a task earns tokens by its `level`. Defaults, in a new `ruleset` value (name is the tech lead's choice):

  | `level` | Tokens |
  | --- | --- |
  | `low` | 1 |
  | `mid` | 2 |
  | `high` | 4 |

- The amount is **fixed at completion**: a later change to the ruleset never changes tokens already earned.
- A task with a question earns its tokens only on "yes". "Undo" within the 4 seconds earns nothing. A retired task earns nothing.
- **A completed task no longer produces a reward.** It produces tokens only.
- **A child's self-created ad hoc task gets `level` `low`** (it was `mid`), so it earns the low value: 1 token by default. This closes the loop where a child could earn more from tasks they invent than from the tasks they are given.
- Adults earn and spend tokens like everyone else.
- **Every balance starts at 0** when 0.9 is installed. Tasks completed before 0.9 earn nothing, because they already produced rewards.
- **[proposal]** On completion, the card shows the tokens earned as icons (one icon per token), not as a number.

## 3. Spending tokens: the `vault`

- **Prices per tier**, in a new `ruleset` value. Defaults: `low` 5, `mid` 12, `high` 25.
- Opening is possible **on any day**: `ruleset.openingDay` is dropped, and with it the weekly opening ritual.
- **The draw:** the reward's `tier` is the one chosen, so `tierOdds` is dropped. The collectible is drawn uniformly among the slots of that tier, **with repeats allowed**. The catalogue is not shown to the children, and nothing says how many collectibles exist.
- **Never below zero:** a person cannot spend more tokens than they have.
- **Spending can never happen twice:** two devices of the same profile, both offline, can never spend the same tokens. This is the exit criterion that replaces "two devices never build two rewards from the same piece". The mechanism (for example, tokens as single-use units with deterministic ids, in the spirit of `SPEC.md` §10) is for the ADR.

### The screen

- **The theme shows depth.** The three tiers are three spots on the `vault` screen, from the entrance (`low`) to the deep end (`high`). Each shows its reward art and its price.
  - The narrative is the theme's. In the pirate theme, tokens are torches, and going deeper costs more torches.
  - The engine only knows three tiers, ordered `low` → `high`. Their placement, art and wording are theme data.
- **The balance:** the active profile's own balance is shown at the top of the `vault` screen, as the token icon and a number. It is shown **only there**: not on the map, and not on any other screen.
  - This is a deliberate exception to "no counts" (`SPEC.md` §2, `CLAUDE.md` rule 6). It is allowed because the number is the person's own and is needed to plan. It is never shown to anyone else.
- **Too few tokens:** a spot the person cannot afford is dimmed, still shows its price, and does nothing when tapped.
- **Opening takes a confirmation.** Tapping an affordable reward selects it and shows "open for N" (with the token icon). Tapping that spends the tokens and opens the reward. Tapping elsewhere cancels.
- **After opening, the collectible is shown and its owner chooses:**
  - **Join the `party`.** With a full `party`, the screen asks who leaves, and the one who leaves goes to the reserve. Once in the `party`, the name label can be set right there.
  - **Let it go:** straight to the reserve (the `showcase`).
  - **Leave without choosing:** the collectible stays pending, as today (`ruleset.pendingDays`), so it is never lost by accident.
- **Names** work as today:
  - in the `party`: the owner can set or change the name, or send the collectible to the reserve;
  - in the reserve: the owner cannot name it and must bring it back into the `party` first (admin moderation in the `showcase` is unchanged).

### Rewards from before 0.9

Unopened rewards from before 0.9 stay whole. They appear in the `vault` as rewards already found, open for free, on any day. There is no migration.

## 4. Configuration (management, admin only)

- Edit the tokens per `level` and the price per `tier`. All are positive integers, and the prices are strictly increasing from `low` to `high`. A change applies to future completions and openings only.
- Edit `partySize` (it exists in the ruleset, but management cannot change it yet).
  - Lowering it never removes anyone from a `party`.
  - A `party` above the new size keeps its members, and nobody can join it until it is below the size.
- `showcaseSize` stays editable as today.
- The `tierOdds` editor goes away.

## 5. Theme package

Every theme, the minimal ones included, must provide:

- the `token` name (singular, plural, gender) and its icon;
- the `vault` layout for the three tiers: position or order, art, and the text that invites going deeper.

`validate-theme` checks both. `scripts/theme-leak-terms.json` gains the pirate token words (`tocha`, `tochas`, `torch`).

## 6. Unchanged

- Retiring a task, as in `SPEC.md` §6.
- The pirate catalogue has at least 8 collectibles per tier. More are added later if repeats start to feel like too many.
- The pending state, the reserve, the `showcase` and "another island".
- Nothing earned is deleted. Spending tokens is a status change, never a deletion.

## 7. Out of 0.9 (radar)

**Three identical collectibles make a "super" one.** This gives repeats a value. Three things are still to be decided:

- whether the three are kept as a new state (stored and hidden, never deleted), which would be a second deliberate exception in reach;
- which collectibles can take part (the `party` and pending only, or also the reserve);
- whether a super one is a generic theme marker or has its own art.

## 9. Exit criteria

- Rules tests for earning, spending and retiring, including the negative cases:
  - spending more than the balance;
  - spending the same tokens twice;
  - a device spending another profile's tokens.
- Two devices of the same profile, both offline, never spend the same tokens, and their balances agree once synced.
- A real family week with tokens, without touching the code.
